About this notice

This notice explains how we collect, use, store and protect the personal information of people who receive care from us, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. If you have any questions, please contact our Data Protection Lead.

Who we are

Ami Home Care Limited (company no. 10744689) is a registered domiciliary care provider regulated by the Care Quality Commission. We deliver personal care and support to adults in their own homes in the London Borough of Hounslow and surrounding areas.

We are the data controller for your personal information. Where we share information with other organisations involved in your care, such as the London Borough of Hounslow or your GP, they are also data controllers in their own right.

Information we collect

  • Personal and contact information: your name, date of birth, address, phone numbers, emergency contacts, next of kin and GP details.
  • Care and health information: your care needs assessment and care plan, relevant medical history, medication records, mental capacity and risk assessments, daily care notes, incident records, and your communication preferences and advance care planning wishes.
  • Financial and administrative information: the London Borough of Hounslow's commissioning reference and your service user agreement.
  • Special category data: some of this information, such as health data, is particularly sensitive and we take extra care to protect it.

How we use your information

We only use your personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so.

PurposeLawful basis
Delivering your care, including planning, scheduling and recording visitsPerformance of a contract, and health or social care purposes
Communicating with you and your emergency contacts about your careLegitimate interests and performance of a contract
Sharing information with health and social care professionals involved in your careHealth or social care purposes, and vital interests where required

How long we keep it

RecordHow long we keep it
Care records and care plans8 years after care ends, or until age 25 if you were under 18 when care began, whichever is longer
Medication administration records8 years
Incident and accident records10 years
Financial and contractual records7 years after the end of the agreement

After that, records are securely deleted or confidentially shredded.

Who we share it with

We only share your information where it is necessary, lawful and proportionate. This may include the London Borough of Hounslow, your GP and other NHS professionals involved in your care, the West and North London Integrated Care Board, pharmacists, hospital discharge teams and the Care Quality Commission.

We use two data processors under signed data processing agreements: Access Group, which provides our digital care records system, and CM2000/Proactis, which provides our electronic call monitoring. We do not sell your information, and we do not transfer it outside the United Kingdom or the European Union.

Keeping it secure

Our measures include role-based access to an NHS-approved digital care records system, password-protected and encrypted devices, multi-factor authentication for remote access, secure connections, confidential shredding of paper records, annual staff training and annual information security reviews. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours and inform you without undue delay.

Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you. We will respond within one calendar month.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to delete your information in certain circumstances, although we may need to keep some records by law.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use your information, or object to certain uses.
  • Receive your information in a portable format where this applies.

We do not make decisions about your care solely by automated means, and we do not carry out direct marketing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Lead.

Using this website

When you send us an enquiry or a job application through this website, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, contact details and anything else you choose to tell us. The form sends these details by email to our office inbox, and we use them only to respond to you.

This website does not use cookies for analytics or advertising, and it does not set any cookies of its own. To display our pages, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and our rating summary from the Care Quality Commission's website.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information, please contact our Data Protection Lead, Agnieszka Menaj, first: email info@amihomecare.com or call 020 3026 3706.

If you remain dissatisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk (opens in a new tab), by calling 0303 123 1113, or by writing to Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF.

Changes to this notice

We review this notice at least once a year and whenever there is a significant change in how we use your information. If we make significant changes, we will tell you directly.

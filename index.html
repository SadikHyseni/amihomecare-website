About Ami Home Care

We sincerely care about your wellbeing.

Ami Home Care provides domiciliary care, also known as home care, to adults in their own homes across the London Borough of Hounslow and nearby areas.

We work closely with every client to provide exactly what they need, and we take the time to understand what each person needs to live well at home.

As much or as little support as you need

  1. One visit a dayA daily call to help with routines
  2. 24-hour careSupport around the clock
  3. Live-in careA matched carer living in your home

Our services

Care that fits your life

Every person is unique, with their own preferences and needs. We provide a range of personal and practical care, tailored to the way you choose to live.

  • Personal Care

    Help with bathing, personal hygiene, getting up and going to bed, so you can stay independent at home.

    Learn more

  • Companionship

    Conversation, company and social support, with practical help such as laundry or shopping.

    Learn more

  • Help at Home Care

    Practical help around the home, such as cleaning, laundry, shopping and preparing meals.

    Learn more

  • Live-in Care

    Care in your own home from a carer we match to you after getting to know you.

    Learn more

  • Night Sitting

    Night sitters who provide support in your own home through the night.

    Learn more

  • Dementia Care

    Support to help older people living with dementia stay in their own home for as long as possible.

    Learn more

  • Complex Care

    Coordinated, person-centred care for people with complex health and social care needs.

    Learn more

  • Hospital Discharge Support

    Support at home while you recover after an illness, surgery or a stay in hospital.

    Learn more

  • Palliative Care

    Specialist palliative care in the comfort of your own home.

    Learn more

Why choose Ami Home Care

Care that comes from the heart.

Choosing care for yourself or someone you love is a big decision. Here's what you can expect from us.

  • Care built around you

    We treat every person as unique and tailor care to the life you choose: when you want it, where you want it and how you want it.

  • Carers matched to you

    For live-in care, we get to know you first and then match a carer to you.

  • Clients who stay with us

    So many of our clients become regulars, and we have continued to grow year after year since 2017.

  • We invest in our carers

    Our care workers can gain fully funded NVQ Level 2 or 3 qualifications in Health and Social Care.

Our commitment

Ami Home Care Limited is committed to achieving net zero emissions by 2050 across its UK operations. As an interim target, we aim to reduce our emissions by 48% by 2030 against our 2024 baseline. We also support the London Borough of Hounslow's ambition to reach net zero by 2030, by reducing the emissions linked to the care we deliver in the borough.

Our emissions

Figures are in tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent (tCO2e). The plan covers our office in Isleworth, our care workers' and managers' travel, and the relevant supply chain.

Emissions2024 baseline2025 (estimated)
Scope 1 (office gas)1.201.20

Total45.0027.27

The two years were calculated using different methods, so we do not claim the difference as a verified reduction. From 2026 we will use the same method each year so that progress can be measured on a like-for-like basis.

Our targets

  • 2027: mileage recording in place for drivers, office energy data requested from our landlord, and our staff travel survey repeated.
  • 2030: 23.40 tCO2e or lower, a 48% reduction against our 2024 baseline.
  • 2050: net zero emissions across our UK operations.

What we are doing

  • Geographical rostering: as we grow, we organise care workers by area so they work close to where they live.
  • Sustainable travel: we encourage walking, cycling and public transport. Our 2026 travel review found around 76% of surveyed staff already travel this way.
  • Digital-first working: electronic care planning, rostering, medication records and online care worker folders reduce paper and printing.
  • Waste: office recycling and a secure confidential shredding service.
  • Sustainable procurement: environmental sustainability is considered when we choose suppliers, alongside quality, infection prevention and cost.
  • Staff awareness: every new member of staff receives environmental awareness information at induction.
